Kindle Dash

A personal e-ink dashboard for an old Kindle.

Kindle Dash turns a jailbroken Kindle into a desk display showing the time, date, weather, Google Calendar events and Google Tasks, refreshed every minute. Events and tasks can be opened on the touchscreen, tasks can be marked as done, and items can be deleted.

It is a private, single-user project. A small server on the owner's own computer reads the owner's calendars and tasks through Google's APIs and draws the dashboard image that the Kindle displays on the home network.

It is not offered as a service to anyone else.
